Privacy policy

GTA 6 Shorts Uploader – last updated 17 November 2026

GTA 6 Shorts Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool the owner of the Cenat Afterhours YouTube channel uses to upload videos to that channel only. Nobody else can use it.

What data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app uses one YouTube Data API permission:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): uploads videos prepared by the channel owner to their own channel, set to private.

The app does not read the channel's videos, comments, statistics or any other Google account data.

How data is used and stored

The sign-in token and a list of uploaded clip IDs are stored locally on the channel owner's own computer, in files only that user can read. The app has no server, and it does not sell, share, transfer or advertise with any data.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Local files are kept until the channel owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which the stored token no longer works.
