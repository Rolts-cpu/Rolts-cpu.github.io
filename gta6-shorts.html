GTA 6 Shorts Uploader

GTA 6 Shorts Uploader is a private tool the RoltsClips channel owner uses to post short GTA 6 clips to the RoltsClips YouTube channel. Clips come only from streamers who have given permission, and every video credits and links the original streamer.

The tool uploads videos to the channel owner's own YouTube account as private videos, which the owner reviews before publishing. It is not offered to other users.
