Privacy policy

Hermes YouTube Uploader – last updated 31 October 2026

Hermes YouTube Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool the owner of the RoltsClips YouTube channel uses to manage that channel only. Nobody else can use it.

What data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app accesses the RoltsClips YouTube account through the YouTube Data API, with these permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): uploads videos chosen by the channel owner, set to private.
  • Read channel and video information (youtube.readonly): checks which channel is connected, upload and processing status, and view, like and comment counts.
  • Manage comments (youtube.force-ssl): posts one prepared comment from the channel on the channel's own public videos, and checks whether the channel has already commented.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only for the purposes above. Sign-in tokens, upload records and statistics are stored locally on the channel owner's own computer, in files only that user can read. The app has no server, and it does not sell, share, transfer or advertise with any data. No human other than the channel owner reads the data.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Local records are kept until the channel owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After access is revoked, the stored tokens no longer work.
